pymodaq_plugins_moke documentation

PyMoDAQ plugins for a magneto-optical Kerr effect (MOKE) bench. This site documents the simulated MOKE bench (plugins MokeMagnetSim, MokeWavePlateSim, MokeSampleRotationSim, MokeGoniometerSim and MokeBalancedSim), which runs scans and gives realistic hysteresis loops without any hardware.
